Why Lake Norman

The lake is the reason a ride matters.

  1. Spread along the water

    Coves, peninsulas, and two-lane roads. A trip from a dock to dinner, or from one shore town to another, still usually means a car.

  2. Visitors who do not know the roads

    Boaters, renters, and weekend traffic pass through Cornelius, Davidson, Mooresville, Denver, and the western coves. Many of them should not need to rent a car for a Saturday.

  3. Next to Charlotte, short on transit

    The lake towns sit on the edge of a large metro and remain hard to cross without driving. A point-to-point autonomous ride would fit the way people already move here.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is this site?

A personal advocacy page. People who live around Lake Norman or visit it can say they want autonomous robotaxi service here. It is not a company, not a ride app, and not a way to reserve a vehicle.

Is this affiliated with the company that builds Cybercab?

No. Lake Norman Cybercab is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Tesla, Inc. or any Tesla product. An individual resident made this page to gather local interest.

What happens to my email?

It goes to the person who published this page, at the address in the form, so they can see who wants service and send an occasional update. It is not sold, and it is not passed to a car company. Reply to that email if you want your note removed.

Will showing interest bring service here?

No one on this page can promise routes, dates, or vehicles. A visible count of residents and visitors is the useful thing this site can do.
