Building a Cybercab fleet on Lake Norman.

I’m planning to buy a Cybercab or two and put them to work around the lake — point-to-point rides between the shore towns and the Charlotte metro. This page is that project. It does not book a ride.

Why Lake Norman

Why a fleet belongs here.

  1. Spread along the water

    Coves and two-lane roads. A trip from a dock to dinner still means a car.

  2. Visitors who do not know the roads

    Cornelius, Davidson, Mooresville, Denver, and the western coves. People come through who do not know these roads.

  3. Next to Charlotte, short on transit

    Point-to-point autonomous rides fit how people already move here.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is this site?

A personal page for a planned small Cybercab fleet on Lake Norman. Not a company pitch deck, not a ride app, not a way to reserve a vehicle today.

Is this affiliated with Tesla?

No. Lake Norman Cybercab is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Tesla, Inc. or any Tesla product. This is a personal project by a local resident.

Can I book a ride?

Not yet. There is no service running from this site.

When?

When vehicles are available and the local operation is ready — this page is the project marker, not a launch date.
